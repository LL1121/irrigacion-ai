Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

This policy describes how Irrigación Bot (the institutional virtual assistant of the Irrigation District Office of Malargüe, Mendoza, Argentina) handles personal data when you use https://ia.irrigacionmalargue.net, including when you connect your Google account.

1. Data controller

The service is operated by Jefatura de Zona de Riego de Malargüe (Irrigación Malargüe) for internal water-management and office work. Contact: ia@irrigacionmalargue.net.

2. Data we collect

Account and identity (Google Sign-In)

  • Google account identifier (sub), email address, name and profile photo.

Google services you authorize (only after you sign in and grant permission)

  • Calendar: read upcoming events and create events you request.
  • Gmail: list or summarize recent messages, and send email only when you ask and confirm.
  • Drive: search and read files you point to, or index a file as assistant context if you ask.

Use of the assistant

  • Chat messages, documents you upload, and notes you ask to save as context (personal or irrigation/office).
  • Minimal technical logs (time, session) needed to operate and audit the service.

3. How we use the data

  • To identify you and keep your session in Irrigación Bot.
  • To run the Calendar, Gmail or Drive actions you request.
  • To remember office-wide irrigation context or personal context, as you choose.
  • To keep the product safe (for example, human confirmation before sending email or creating an event).

We do not sell data. We do not use Gmail, Calendar or Drive content for advertising, or to train third-party AI models for commercial purposes.

4. Limited Use of Google user data

Access to Google APIs complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy , including the Limited Use requirements for Gmail:

  • We use this data only to provide and improve the user-facing features you trigger.
  • We do not transfer Google user data to third parties except as needed to run our own infrastructure, to comply with law, or when you explicitly instruct us (for example, send an email).
  • Humans do not read Gmail, Drive or Calendar content except: (a) with your specific consent, (b) for security / abuse investigation, (c) to comply with law, or (d) when data is aggregated and no longer identifies a person.

5. How we store the data

  • Google access and refresh tokens are stored encrypted in our database.
  • The app session uses a signed cookie or JWT, with a configurable expiry (default 7 days).
  • Irrigation/office context may be used by the assistant for office staff; personal context is tied to your user account.

6. Sharing

We do not share your Google data with other organizations. Strictly necessary hosting providers under Irrigación Malargüe control may process it (server and database). Google processes sign-in and API calls under Google’s own policies.

7. Retention and deletion

  • You can sign out of Irrigación Bot at any time.
  • You can revoke the app’s access from your Google Account → Third-party access.
  • To request deletion of your user, tokens and personal context, email ia@irrigacionmalargue.net.

8. Confirmations and security

Write actions (create an event, send email) require your authorization in the UI (Authorize / Cancel), unless you already approved that same tool. Access is intended for authorized staff of the Irrigation District Office.

9. Your rights

You may request access, correction or deletion of personal data associated with Irrigación Bot by contacting ia@irrigacionmalargue.net. Rights under Argentine Law 25.326 on Personal Data Protection also apply.

10. Changes

If we update this policy, we will publish the new version at https://irrigacionmalargue.net/politicas-privacidad and change the “Last updated” date.
